## 2. Tier 1: Knockout Gates (Binary Disqualification Rules)

A market must pass **all four knockout gates** before receiving resources for in-depth financial and operational modeling. Failing any single gate results in immediate disqualification or postponement.

| Gate ID | Strategic Gate | Quantitative / Qualitative Boundary | Consulting Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **KO-1** | Minimum Addressable Scale | **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) $\ge \$50\text{M}$ USD** | If the mid-market connected fitness SAM is below $50M, the country cannot support the localized inventory, regulatory, and marketing overhead required to reach profitability. |
| **KO-2** | Tariff & Trade Barrier Ceiling | **Effective landed tariff and customs duty $\le 18\%$** | Duties above 18% directly cannibalize hardware gross margins below the minimum viable contribution threshold. |
| **KO-3** | Heavy Logistics Infrastructure | **Proven, operational two-person 3PL network capable of handling 40kg+ home delivery** | Delivering 40kg+ equipment via standard parcel carriers results in unacceptable transit damage (>10%), delivery refusals, and brand damage. |
| **KO-4** | Regulatory Compliance Viability | **Product redesign or safety re-certification lead time $\le 6\text{ months}$ & cost $\le \$150\text{k}$** | Excessive regulatory barriers delay market launch past the Board's FY2026 strategic window. |

## 3. Tier 2: Weighted Multi-Factor Scoring Matrix

Candidate markets that pass all Tier 1 gates are scored on a standardized **1.0 (Poorest) to 5.0 (Optimal)** scale across three core pillars:

### Proposed Weighting Distribution (Assumption)

> [!NOTE]
> **Explicit Assumption Label**: The weighting distribution below represents the baseline management assumption established with the executive team. 
> * **Market Attractiveness**: **35%** (Assumption)
> * **Economic Viability**: **40%** (Assumption)
> * **Execution Feasibility**: **25%** (Assumption)
> * **Total**: **100%**

### Why the Weights May Be Changed (Sensitivity & Governance Rationale)

The baseline weighting reflects NovaHome’s current corporate profile: a growth-stage company seeking sustainable international revenue while protecting near-term cash runway. However, these weights may be adjusted based on strategic priorities:

1. **Shift to Capital Preservation (Risk-Averse Scenario)**:
   * *Adjusted Weights*: Economic Viability $\rightarrow$ **50%**, Execution Feasibility $\rightarrow$ **30%**, Market Attractiveness $\rightarrow$ **20%**.
   * *Rationale*: If domestic cash flows weaken further or interest rates rise, management must prioritize rapid cash break-even and low operational friction over sheer market size.
2. **Shift to Aggressive Land-Grab (Venture/Growth Scenario)**:
   * *Adjusted Weights*: Market Attractiveness $\rightarrow$ **50%**, Economic Viability $\rightarrow$ **30%**, Execution Feasibility $\rightarrow$ **20%**.
   * *Rationale*: If a well-capitalized competitor threatens to lock up international market share, NovaHome may prioritize establishing a large installed base in high-volume markets, accepting a longer payback window.
3. **Shift to Supply Chain Bottleneck (Operational Constraint Scenario)**:
   * *Adjusted Weights*: Execution Feasibility $\rightarrow$ **40%**, Economic Viability $\rightarrow$ **35%**, Market Attractiveness $\rightarrow$ **25%**.
   * *Rationale*: If global freight disruptions or tariff volatility spike, operational ease, existing logistics lanes, and regulatory simplicity become the primary determinant of success.

## 5. Mathematical Formulas for Quantitative Metrics

Every quantitative metric utilized in our financial models and scoring tables is defined mathematically:

### 1. Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR)
$$\text{CAGR} = \left( \frac{\text{Value}_{t_n}}{\text{Value}_{t_0}} \right)^{\frac{1}{n}} - 1$$
* **Application**: Category market size growth from 2026 to 2029 ($n = 3$).

### 2. Delivered Hardware Contribution Margin ($M_c$)
$$M_c = \frac{\text{Net Revenue} - (\text{FOB COGS} + \text{Ocean Freight} + \text{Tariffs} + \text{Inbound Drayage} + \text{3PL Outbound Delivery} + \text{Payment Fee})}{\text{Net Revenue}}$$
* **Hurdle**: Target $M_c \ge 28.0\%$ in Year 1.

### 3. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
$$\text{CAC} = \frac{\text{Total In-Country Digital Ad Spend} + \text{Agency Retainers} + \text{Influencer Fees}}{\text{Total New Hardware Customers Acquired}}$$
* **Hurdle**: Initial $\text{CAC} \le \$260$, scaling down to $\le \$220$ by Year 2.

### 4. Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
$$\text{LTV} = \text{Hardware Contribution} + \sum_{m=1}^{M} \frac{\text{Monthly Subscription Revenue} \times \text{Software Gross Margin} \times (1 - \text{Churn Rate})^m}{(1 + r)^m}$$
* **Hurdle**: $\text{LTV} / \text{CAC} \ge 3.0\times$ by Month 24.

### 5. Payback Period
$$\text{Payback Period} = t \quad \text{such that} \quad \sum_{m=1}^{t} \text{Net Operating Cash Flow}_m \ge \text{Cumulative Initial Investment Outlay}$$
* **Hurdle**: $\le 18\text{ Months}$ from commercial launch.

### 6. Internal Rate of Return (IRR)
$$\text{NPV} = \sum_{t=0}^{T} \frac{\text{Cash Flow}_t}{(1 + \text{IRR})^t} = 0$$
* **Hurdle**: 3-Year Project $\text{IRR} \ge 15.0\%$.

## 6. Final Decision Classification & Governance

Following completion of the analysis in Phase 04 through Phase 07, markets will be categorized into three strategic tiers:

* **Tier 1: Priority Expansion Candidates (Green Light)**:
  * Pass all Knockout Gates.
  * Composite Weighted Score $\ge 3.75 / 5.00$.
  * Financial Model confirms Payback $\le 18\text{ months}$ and $\text{IRR} \ge 15.0\%$.
* **Tier 2: Phased / Secondary Candidates (Yellow Light)**:
  * Pass all Knockout Gates.
  * Composite Weighted Score between $3.20$ and $3.74$.
  * Recommended for secondary wave (2027+) or contingent upon retail partnership distribution.
* **Tier 3: Disqualified Markets (Red Light)**:
  * Fail one or more Knockout Gates OR Composite Weighted Score $< 3.20$.
  * Documented in the Rejection Register with explicit operational and economic rationales.
